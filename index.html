What is Filipino martial arts?

Eskrima, Kali, Arnis — the weapon-based fighting arts of the Philippines, forged over centuries and passed down hand to hand.

It begins with the stick and the blade, and grows into empty hands — one complete system. The same angles, footwork, and timing carry through whether you're holding a weapon or not, so everything you learn builds on everything else.

Training is built around flow: striking, blocking, disarming, and countering in continuous, living motion. You learn to read an opponent, to move with economy and purpose, and to answer force with skill — not size or strength.

That is the heart of it: skill and strategy that can equalize an encounter, letting a smaller person stand with confidence against a larger one. It is an art anyone can do and enjoy — as gentle or rough as one desires — and a practice that sharpens the mind as much as the body.
